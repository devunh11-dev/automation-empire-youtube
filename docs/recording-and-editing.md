# Authentic Face-and-Voice Recording Guide

## Before recording
- Clean the camera lens; use the rear camera if framing can be monitored, otherwise front camera is fine.
- Record vertically (9:16), at 1080p or higher if the phone supports it.
- Put the lens at eye level. Frame head and upper torso with a little space above the head.
- Face a window or soft light; avoid a bright window behind you.
- Record in a quiet room. Put the phone close enough for clear speech; use a wired mic if already available, but do not buy gear just for this workflow.
- Record 2 seconds of silence and one full take without stopping.

## Performance
- Speak to one person, not a crowd. Start with the hook immediately.
- Use your natural voice and normal accent. Energy comes from clarity and pace, not shouting.
- Record one alternate hook and one alternate CTA after the main take.
- Keep your face visible for the hook and key takeaway. Use screen recording/B-roll only to illustrate the workflow.

## Edit recipe
- Canvas: 1080×1920, 9:16.
- First frame: face visible and a readable 3–6 word hook.
- Cut pauses, repeated words and dead air; keep natural breaths where useful.
- Add accurate burned-in captions, 1–2 lines at a time, high contrast and inside mobile safe areas.
- Use blue/gold accents sparingly for emphasis; keep skin tones natural and text readable.
- Alternate face shot and relevant screen capture/B-roll every 2–4 seconds when it improves comprehension.
- Keep music low enough that every word is clear; use only owned or licensed audio.
- End with one simple CTA and a clean final frame.

## Export and final review
- MP4, H.264 video, AAC audio, 1080×1920, 30 fps (or match the source frame rate).
- Watch the full export on a phone with sound on and captions visible.
- Check first 2 seconds, voice clarity, caption accuracy, safe margins, crop, spelling, music rights and ending.
- Save the source recording and final export separately. Never overwrite the original.

## Asset naming
`AE_YYYY-MM-DD_topic_take01.mp4` for source; `AE_YYYY-MM-DD_topic_short_v01.mp4` for export; keep thumbnail and caption files with the same topic slug.
