# ChatGPT Production Prompts

## 1. Research a video idea
“Research this topic for an Automation Empire YouTube Short: [topic]. Find current, reliable sources. Give me 3 original angles for beginner creators. Separate verified facts from opinions and unknowns. Include source links, limitations, and one demonstration I can record myself. Do not copy another creator.”

## 2. Write a 27-second script
“Write a natural 27-second YouTube Short for my own face and real voice. Audience: beginner creators and solo operators. Topic: [topic]. Use hook → context → one demonstrable action → takeaway → one CTA. Aim for 48–62 spoken words. Keep my authentic speaking voice; no generic AI presenter tone. Do not invent results.”

## 3. Build a shot list
“Turn this approved script into a phone-friendly 9:16 shot list. Keep my face on camera for the hook and takeaway; use screen recordings only where they prove the step. Give timestamps, captions, B-roll and edit notes. Preserve my real appearance and voice.”

## 4. Package the upload
“Create 3 accurate title options under 100 characters, a concise description, 5–8 relevant tags, one pinned-comment question and a thumbnail brief. Avoid clickbait that overstates the demonstration. Keep the Automation Empire blue/gold visual direction.”

## 5. Repurpose without duplicating blindly
“Adapt this approved video for Instagram Reels and TikTok. Keep the underlying demonstration truthful, but rewrite the opening caption and CTA for each platform. Do not imply it has been published or performed well unless I provide those results.”

## 6. Review the final cut
“Review this video against the brief: first-two-second hook, authentic face/voice, clarity, caption accuracy, pacing, evidence, safe-area layout, audio, rights and CTA. Return timestamped fixes and a pass/fail checklist. Do not infer facts that are not visible or audible.”
